!=============================================================================
! switch-200V-LT2-1 - add new VLANs to the trunks to LT2-2 and LT2-3
! Ruckus ICX8200-24P, FastIron 10.0.10g
! Done ahead of the main 200V cutover
!=============================================================================
!
! Why
!   LT2-2 and LT2-3 hang off LT2-1 (confirmed by LLDP):
!     LT2-1 1/1/23  <->  switch-200V-LT2-2 1/1/12
!     LT2-1 1/1/24  <->  switch-200V-LT2-3 1/1/12
!   Both switches already have the new VLANs tagged on their 1/1/12, but
!   LT2-1 1/1/23 and 1/1/24 only carry the old VLANs (100, 1212, 1213, 1230,
!   1240, 1250, 1271, 1272, 1273). This change tags the 22 new VLANs on both
!   ports so LT2-2 and LT2-3 can reach the core on them.
!
! What changes
!   Adds tagged membership only. All 22 VLANs already exist on LT2-1 and are
!   already tagged on its uplink to the core (lag 10). Nothing is removed and
!   no untagged VLAN changes, so existing traffic on the old VLANs is not
!   touched. 2311 and 2321 are not added (not used on LT2-2 or LT2-3).
!
! Expected impact
!   None for current users. Spanning tree runs per VLAN, so only the newly
!   added VLANs go through listening/learning (about 30 seconds) on these two
!   ports. LT2-2 and LT2-3 have no devices on the new VLANs yet.
!
! New VLANs added (tagged on 1/1/23 and 1/1/24)
!   2000  VL-netmgmt-01
!   2010  VL-wifimgmt-01
!   2020  VL-servers-01
!   2100  VL-staff-wired-01
!   2200  VL-student-wired-01
!   2300  VL-wifi-01
!   2310  VL-wifi-staff-01
!   2320  VL-wifi-student-01
!   2400  VL-byod-01
!   2500  VL-iot-01
!   2510  VL-bac-01
!   2520  VL-bms-01
!   2530  VL-library-01
!   2600  VL-cctv-01
!   2700  VL-av-01
!   2800  VL-print-01
!   2810  VL-comms-01
!   2900  VL-guest-01
!   2950  VL-nac-restricted-01
!   2951  VL-nac-onboard-01
!   2952  VL-nac-remediate-01
!   2953  VL-nac-fallback-01
!=============================================================================
