!=============================================================================
! 200V VLAN migration - unlisted AV devices (D8:43:AE:B1) from 1230 to 2700
! Ruckus ICX, FastIron 10.0.10g
! Source: 200V Device Port List.xlsx, Review tab items 26, 27, 35-42, 44, 47, 50-53
!=============================================================================
!
! Scope: untagged VLAN on 16 access ports across 2 switches
!   switch-200V-stack-lv3       192.168.122.22      8 ports
!   switch-200V-stack-lv4       192.168.122.21      8 ports
!
! VLAN mapping
!   1230 (200V_av) -> 2700 (VL-av-01)  16 ports
!
! Why: 16 devices with near-consecutive MACs D8:43:AE:B1:xx:xx, one per room on
! the old AV VLAN 1230 and not in the device list. The rest of each room's AV
! moved to 2700 in the 9 Oct cutover, so these move to 2700 to join it.
!
! Before starting
!   - VLAN 2700 exists on lv3 and lv4 and is tagged on lag 10 (used by the
!     cutover). The core has ve 2700 (10.21.36.0/23) with DHCP helpers
!   - Pre-check each port's MAC: if a port shows a different MAC, or the AV
!     team says a device must stay on 1230, leave that port's lines out
!
! Same method as the cutover: CHANGE removes 1230 first, then adds 2700 untagged.
! Tagged VLANs are not touched. PORT BOUNCE makes DHCP devices renew; it does
! not remove PoE. 2700 runs spanning-tree, so ports go Listen > Learn > Forward
! (about 30 s) after the bounce before MACs are learned.
! Per switch: PRE-CHECK > CHANGE > PORT BOUNCE > VERIFY > write memory.
!=============================================================================
