!=============================================================================
! 200V access switches - remove 'management source-interface ve 100 protocol all'
! Ruckus ICX, FastIron 10.0.10g
!=============================================================================
!
! Switches with the line (from their current configs):
!   switch-200V-stack-lv3      ve 100 = 192.168.122.22
!   switch-200V-stack-lv6      ve 100 = 192.168.122.11
!   switch-200V-LT2-1          ve 100 = 192.168.122.12
!   switch-200V-LT2-2          ve 100 = 192.168.122.13
!   switch-200V-8              ve 100 = 192.168.122.16
! Not present on switch-200V-stack-lv4, switch-200V-LT2-3 or
! switch-200V-Tutorial-6.03, so nothing to do there.
! trinity200V-core uses 'management source-interface ve 1960 protocol all';
! that is not VE 100 and is not changed here.
!
! What changes
!   The line sets the source IP for traffic the switch starts itself (SNMP
!   traps, syslog, NTP, TFTP, DNS and similar). Each of these switches has
!   only one IP address, on ve 100, so that traffic keeps the same source
!   address. Inbound SSH/telnet to the switch is not affected.
!
! Do one switch at a time. Keep the current session open until the verify
! step has passed.
!=============================================================================

!=============================================================================
! switch-200V-stack-lv3  (192.168.122.22)
!=============================================================================
!
!-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! PRE-CHECK
!-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
show running-config | include management source-interface
show ntp associations
!
!-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! CHANGE
!-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
configure terminal
no management source-interface ve 100 protocol all
end
!
!-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! VERIFY
!-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! Line should be gone (no output)
show running-config | include management source-interface
! NTP still synchronised; open a new SSH/telnet session to confirm access
show ntp associations
write memory
!
!-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
! ROLLBACK
!-----------------------------------------------------------------------------
configure terminal
management source-interface ve 100 protocol all
end
write memory
!
