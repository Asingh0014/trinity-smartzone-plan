!=============================================================================
! 200V VLAN migration - outlets with 20:7B:D2:30 devices, 1230 to 2100
! Ruckus ICX, FastIron 10.0.10g
! Source: device MAC/outlet list, ports from Switch Physical Patching - Dycom
!   2025-07-30.xlsx (Level 3 and Level 4 sheets, Type = AV)
!=============================================================================
!
! Scope: untagged VLAN on 13 access ports across 2 switches
!   switch-200V-stack-lv3       192.168.122.22      7 ports
!   switch-200V-stack-lv4       192.168.122.21      6 ports
!
! VLAN mapping
!   1230 (200V_av) -> 2100 (VL-staff-wired-01)  13 ports
!
! Why: 16 devices (MACs 20:7B:D2:30:xx:xx) were given with their outlet
! labels, to go on VLAN 2100 (decision 9 Oct). The patching sheet puts 13 on
! ports still untagged on 1230 with no device in the device list, so the
! cutover left them there. The other 3 outlets (200V3J14 lv3 2/1/26, 200V4I17
! lv4 1/1/24, 200V4H9 lv4 2/1/24) are on ports now on 2700 where the device
! list has other devices (Touch Panel, Airtames); not in this file - confirm on site.
!
! Before starting
!   - The patching sheet is from July 2025 and may be out of date. Most of
!     these ports were down on 9 Oct, so the MACs could not be confirmed on
!     the switch. Check each port in the pre-check: if a port is up with a
!     MAC that is not 207b.d230.xxxx, leave that port's lines out
!   - VLAN 2100 exists on lv3 and lv4, tagged on lag 10, with spanning-tree.
!     The core has ve 2100 (10.21.3.0/24) with DHCP helpers
!
! Same method as the cutover: CHANGE removes 1230 first, then adds 2100 untagged.
! Tagged VLANs are not touched. PORT BOUNCE makes DHCP devices renew; it does
! not remove PoE. 2100 runs spanning-tree, so ports go Listen > Learn > Forward
! (about 30 s) after the bounce before MACs are learned. A port that is down
! needs no bounce: its device comes up on 2100 when it next connects.
! Per switch: PRE-CHECK > CHANGE > PORT BOUNCE > VERIFY > write memory.
!=============================================================================
